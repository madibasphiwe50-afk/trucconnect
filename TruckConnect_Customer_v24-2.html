FAQ
How does TruckConnect work? ▼
Post your load, pick a truck size, and we match you with a nearby driver. You pay the driver directly in cash after delivery.
How do I cancel a booking? ▼
Open your active booking and tap the Cancel button. If a driver has already accepted, please contact them via chat before cancelling.
How is the fare calculated? ▼
Fares are estimated based on distance and truck size. The final amount is agreed between you and the driver. All payments are cash on delivery.
What if my driver doesn't arrive? ▼
Use the chat to contact your driver first. If there's no response, cancel the booking and a new driver will be matched. Contact support if you need help.
How do I report a driver? ▼
After your trip is completed, tap "Report Driver" on your booking card and select a reason. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours.
